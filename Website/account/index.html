Account

Your Latitude ATC account

One Discord sign in covers applying, joining the server, and seeing which parts of the site you are cleared for.

Sign in with Discord

We ask Discord for your username, avatar, and the roles you hold on our server. Nothing is posted anywhere, and we never see your password.

Discord is asked for identify and guilds.members.read only. Both are read-only, and either can be revoked from your Discord settings at any time.

Sign in with Discord

You can read everything on this site without signing in. An account is only needed to apply, to join the queue, or to view the Radar.

What you can do

  • Apply for a position

    Your application is tied to this account, so staff can reply to you on Discord.

  • Join the queue

    Request an invite to the server and get it straight away.

  • Apply for ATC

    You hold the Pilot role, so the controller application is open to you.

  • View the Radar

    You hold the controller role, so the live board is available to you.

Roles are read from Discord

Everything on your account is derived from the roles you hold on our Discord server. We do not keep a separate copy of your permissions, so promoting or demoting someone takes effect here immediately.
