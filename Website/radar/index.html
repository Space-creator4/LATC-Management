Reading the board

Sectors stay put, people move

The layout is fixed so you can learn where things are. Controllers signing on and off is the only thing that changes, which means a gap in the pattern is a real gap rather than a reshuffle.

A name means staffed

Every controller on position is listed by callsign, with their role underneath.

Unstaffed is useful information

If your sector shows unstaffed, that is where a controller would help most.

Positions come from Discord

On-position status is read from the roles staff assign you. It cannot be edited from the website.

Want to be on the board?

The path in is the ATC application.

ATC applications are open to approved pilots only. If you have not applied yet, start with the pilot application, and the controller route opens once you are accepted.

Pilot application ATC application

Already a pilot? The ATC application opens once you sign in, because your role is what unlocks it.

Not a controller yet?

The Radar is one of the things you get to use once you are on the team. Everything else about the network is open to you now.
