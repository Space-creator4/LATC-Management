Before You Write

Quick answers

These cover most of what people ask us. If your question is not here, send it through and we will help.

How long does an application take?

Applications are reviewed by staff in their own time. If yours has not been looked at after a reasonable period, feel free to ask in Discord.

Do I need aviation experience to control?

No. Prior ATC experience is welcome, but not required. Training covers the position, our procedures and how we run sessions.

Can I change role later?

Yes. Talk to a staff member about applying for a different position. Many members fly and control at different times.

Why can't we roleplay an emergency?

Simulated MAYDAY, PAN and other emergency scenarios are prohibited. Sessions work best when everyone knows what is real and what is standard traffic.

Discord is the quickest route

Most questions get answered in minutes in our Discord. Applications, support and reports all start there.
