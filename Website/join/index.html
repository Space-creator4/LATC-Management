Why a queue?

Because invites have to be released, not published

A Discord invite link sitting in a page's source is a link anyone can copy. Serving it from the backend means the only way to see it is to ask the service for it, which ties the invite to an account and lets us revoke access when someone needs it removed.

Approval is automatic

There is no staff step to wait for. You are added and the link comes back in the same response.

The link is per member

Your invite belongs to your Discord account. Someone else asking gets their own, and we can revoke one without disturbing everybody else.

Roles arrive with you

Joining through here means the service already knows your Discord ID, so your pilot and controller roles are picked up as soon as staff give them.

Once you are in

Three things worth doing early.

  1. Read the rules

    Ten rules, short enough to read properly. They cover the things that actually get people into trouble.

  2. Verify your Roblox account

    Run the verification command in the server. It links your Roblox account to your Discord one. Staff will never ask for your password.

  3. Apply for a position

    Applications are on this site and tied to the account you just signed in with.

The rules Applications

Do not share your invite link

Your invite is linked to your account. If it ends up somewhere public, tell a member of staff so it can be replaced.

Questions first?

Ask in the Discord before you commit to anything. Someone will usually reply quickly.
