Rulebook

Short, clear, and non-negotiable

Ten rules cover everything we ask of members on the network. They exist to keep sessions safe, fair and enjoyable for pilots and controllers alike.

Jump to the rules Open the full rulebook

General Rules

Applies to everyone

These rules apply to every member of the community, on and off the network, without exception.

Follow ATC instructions

Any instruction given by an authorised controller must be followed. If an instruction is unclear or unsafe, ask before continuing.

General

No fake ATC

Only authorised controllers may issue instructions. Impersonating a control position or giving orders you have no authority to give is not permitted.

General

Pilot Rules

Applies to pilots

For members who hold an approved pilot application and fly on the network.

Follow clearances

Comply with every clearance you receive, including taxi, hold and departure instructions. Never act on an instruction you have not been given.

Pilot

No intentional collisions

Intentionally causing, allowing or staging a collision with another aircraft is strictly prohibited.

Pilot

No runway abuse

Do not take off, land, taxi or occupy a runway without the required clearance, and do not block a runway used by other traffic.

Pilot

ATC Rules

Applies to controllers

For members who are authorised to work an air traffic control position.

Controllers must be authorised

You may only work a position for which you hold current authorisation. Authorisation can be suspended at any time.

ATC

Game Rules

Applies to everyone in-game

These rules govern behaviour inside the Roblox experience itself.

No emergency scenarios

Emergency scenarios are not permitted. This includes simulated MAYDAY and PAN calls, medical or mechanical emergencies, and any emergency used as roleplay. No exceptions.

Prohibited

No intentional disruptions

Do not disrupt other members' sessions through trolling, spamming, deliberate radio interference, or repeatedly interfering with traffic.

Game

No exploiting

Using exploits, glitches, scripts or unintended game mechanics to gain an advantage is forbidden, as is exploiting bugs in the community's systems.

Game

Community Rules

Applies to all community spaces

Our Discord and other community spaces follow the same expectations as the network.

Respect other players

Treat every member with respect, in-game and in our community spaces. Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks have no place on the network or in Discord.

Community

Emergency scenarios are not permitted

To be completely clear: no member may roleplay a simulated emergency of any kind, including MAYDAY and PAN. This is one of the strictest rules on the network and staff will enforce it.

Breaking a rule

Staff may issue a warning, remove a member from a session, or suspend your application depending on the severity of the breach. Repeated breaches can end your membership of the network.

Read it, understood it, ready to apply?

Every application asks whether you have read the rules. Come to Discord if anything is unclear before you submit.

The rules in short

  • Follow instructions from authorised ATC.
  • Never cause or stage a collision.
  • Work only positions you are authorised for.
  • Never roleplay an emergency scenario.
