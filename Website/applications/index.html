Join The Network

Pick your role and apply

There are three ways into Latitude ATC. Choose the one that fits you, read the requirements, then complete the application on its own page.

The Process

Four steps from interest to network

The process is deliberately straightforward. Most applications are reviewed within a reasonable time by our staff team.

Read the rules

Every applicant confirms they have read and understood the rulebook before applying.

Submit the form

Complete the application for your chosen role on this page. Answer honestly — it helps us place you correctly.

Verification

Once approved, verify your Roblox account by running the verification command in Roblox so we can link it.

Training

Join training for your role. Pilots get a network introduction; controllers work supervised before signing off.

Requirements

What we need from you

There are no aviation qualifications required. These are the practical requirements for each role.

All roles
An active Roblox account, membership of our Discord, acceptance of the rulebook, and a willingness to take direction and feedback.
Pilots
Basic familiarity with flying in Roblox. You will be taught the network's procedures, phraseology and clearances.
Controllers
Clear communication, patience with new pilots, and a good grasp of how aviation works. Prior ATC experience is welcome but not required.
Staff
Experience on the network, a consistent record of following the rules, and the time to commit to reviewing applications and supporting members.
Verification
Your Roblox account must be verifiable. Staff will provide the command to run — never share a password or cookie with anyone, including staff.

Never share your password

No member of Latitude ATC staff will ever ask for your Roblox password or cookies. If someone claiming to be staff asks you for them, report it immediately.

Apply

Choose your application

Each application has its own page, so you only answer the questions that matter for the role you want. Pick one and you are ready to apply.

Pilot Application

For members who want to fly on the network as a pilot. We ask about your flying experience and when you are usually available.

  • Roblox and Discord usernames
  • Flying experience and availability
  • Why you want to join
Start pilot application

ATC Application

For members who want to control the network. No prior ATC experience needed — we ask which position interests you.

  • Roblox and Discord usernames
  • Preferred position and experience
  • Availability and motivation
Start ATC application

Accepted pilots only.

Staff Application

For members who want to help run the community. Reviewed only for members already active on the network.

Currently closed

  • Your history on the network
  • How much time you can commit
  • What you would like to do
Read about staff roles

One application at a time

Submit a single form so staff can review it properly. If you want to change role later, contact us and we will help you apply again.

ATC is for accepted pilots, and staff is closed

ATC applications need an accepted pilot application first. Staff applications are closed until the next batch opens, and we announce that in the Discord.

Not sure which role fits?

Join the Discord and tell us what you are interested in. We will point you towards the right application.
