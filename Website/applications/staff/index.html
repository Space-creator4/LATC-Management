Before You Apply

What staff actually do

Staff applications are only considered from members who are already active on the network. Here is what the role involves.

Review applications

Read pilot and controller applications and make recommendations to the wider staff team.

Train and sign off

Mentor new controllers through training and authorise them once you are satisfied they are ready.

Handle reports

Deal with rule breaches fairly and consistently, and keep records of warnings and suspensions.

Maintain standards

Keep the rulebook accurate and make sure it reflects how the community actually operates.

You need an existing track record

Staff applications are reviewed only for members who are already active and have a consistent record of following the rules. Be patient and build that record first.

Never share your password

No member of Latitude ATC staff will ever ask for your Roblox password or cookies. Report anyone who does.
