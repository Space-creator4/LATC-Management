Before You Apply

How controllers progress

You do not need aviation experience to apply. Choose a preferred position and staff will guide you through training from there.

You need an accepted pilot application

ATC applications are open to existing Latitude ATC pilots only. You will confirm your pilot status at the top of the form, and staff match your application to the pilot record they already have.

01

Ground

Taxiing, parking and departure routing. Where every new controller begins.

02

Local

Departure sequencing and handing traffic to the next position.

03

Tower

Landing clearances, runway coordination and arrivals within the field.

04

Approach

Managing arriving traffic and sequencing aircraft towards the field.

Choosing "no preference yet" is fine

If you are unsure which position suits you, say so. Staff will help you work it out.

Never share your password

No member of Latitude ATC staff will ever ask for your Roblox password or cookies. Report anyone who does.
