Before You Apply

What happens next

Submit the form and your application goes straight to our staff team. There is no Discord application channel to wait in.

Staff review

A member of staff reads your application. They are looking for enthusiasm and reliability, not qualifications.

We contact you

You will get a message on Discord, which is how staff reach every member.

Verification

Once approved, run the verification command in Roblox so we can link your account. Never share your password.

Network introduction

New pilots get a walkthrough of our procedures, phraseology and clearances before joining a full session.

Never share your password

No member of Latitude ATC staff will ever ask for your Roblox password or cookies. Report anyone who does.
